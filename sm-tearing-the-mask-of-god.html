Synopsis

从很久以前，因特拉肯城中便流传着一则关于「弑乐园」的传说。

相传只要在「弑乐园」中完成献给神明的死亡表演，便能向无所不能的神明许愿，达成心底的愿望，万事皆可。届时神明会降下红云，此为兑现诺言的凭证。

岁月轮转，日月如梭，在漫长的历史变迁中，无数的高楼大厦在因特拉肯平地而起，钢铁组成的机器也在其中运转。而这则古老的传说也成了茶余饭后的笑谈。

直到今日，一个佝偻着背的老人再一次来到了神面前。这是近百年之中，扣响门扉的第二位访客。

而神明也在此等待了男人许久……

For as long as anyone can remember, a legend has circulated through the city of Interlaken — the legend of the "Slaughter Paradise."

It is said that anyone who performs a death offering to the gods within the Slaughter Paradise may make a wish to the all-powerful deity and have any desire granted. When the wish is accepted, the god descends a red cloud as proof of the promise kept.

Time passed, and skyscrapers rose from the flatlands of Interlaken, iron machines humming within them. The ancient legend faded into the background noise of everyday life — something to joke about over tea.

Until today, when a hunched old man came once more before the god. In nearly a century, he is only the second visitor to knock on that door.

And the god has been waiting for him for a very long time…
