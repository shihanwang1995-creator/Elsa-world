Synopsis

无风之境的花园中，为证明阿基里斯悖论而存在的落星馆仍遥遥矗立。无冕侦探手持裁定之枪，目光熠熠地指着面前最后的诡计狂想者。

侦探会亲手杀死诡计，就如同众人期望的一般。那包含甜美的子弹终是射向面前之人的头颅。脸上绽放着甜美的笑意，直至眉心被锐利的子弹贯穿，身躯向后方倾去。

诡计终于迎来了甜美的死亡。

鲜血染红了花园中的白色铃兰，天空中亮起了繁杂神秘的符文。

  • 设定规则之下的审判裁定
  • 逝者携手共赴死亡的果决
  • 击穿宿命遗忘的甜美子弹
  • 构建铭记的第四种可能性
  • 名为「诡计甜美死去」的赌约，此刻正式开始

In the windless garden, the Falling Star Manor — built to prove Achilles' Paradox — still stands tall in the distance. The uncrowned detective holds the Verdict Gun, eyes blazing, pointing it at the last Trick Fantasist before him.

The detective will kill Trick with his own hands, just as everyone expects. The bullet filled with sweetness flies toward the head of the person before him. A sweet smile blooms across their face — until a sharp bullet pierces between the brows, and the body tilts backward.

Trick has finally met a sweet death.

Fresh blood stains the white lilies of the valley in the garden. In the sky, complex and mysterious runes begin to glow.

  • A verdict rendered under established rules
  • The resolve of the departed, walking hand in hand toward death
  • A sweet bullet that pierces through fate's forgetting
  • A fourth possibility, built to be remembered
  • The wager called "Trick's Sweet Death" — now officially begins
